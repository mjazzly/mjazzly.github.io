The operating manual

Three independent assessments, taken years apart, describe the same machine: one that generates options, evaluates them hard, and then builds the chosen one into a system. This page shows the results and, more importantly, how they interlock in real delivery work.

CliftonStrengths: Top 5

Gallup ranks 34 talent themes. These five lead mine, and the order tells a story: two themes that generate, two that filter, one that fuels.

#1

Ideation

Fascinated by ideas and by finding the simple elegant explanation inside complex procedures. In practice: the first person in the room to propose a different way the workflow could run.

#2

Strategic

Sorts through clutter to find the route that matters. In practice: turns Ideation's ten options into the two worth testing, and sees the second-order effects before committing.

#3

Input

Collects, archives, and cross-references. In practice: the reason my playbooks, style guides, metric definitions, and reference documentation exist; knowledge is stored so it can be reused, not just remembered.

#4

Empathy

Reads the emotional state of a room accurately. In practice: stakeholder management that anticipates concerns before they are raised, and dashboards and findings framed around what the audience actually worries about.

#5

Deliberative

Careful, vigilant, risk-aware. In practice: the validation protocol. Nothing ships on optimism; numbers and outputs are checked before they reach a stakeholder, and praise is only given when deserved.

Belbin Team Roles: Top 3

Belbin measures the role a person naturally plays in a team. My profile is unusually consistent with the Gallup result: evaluate first, then build.

Role 1

Monitor Evaluator

Sober, strategic, discerning. Weighs options impartially and judges accurately. The team member who reads the fine print before the team signs.

Role 2

Implementer

Turns decisions into organised, working systems. Disciplined execution of a chosen plan; the bridge between a good idea and a running process.

Role 3

Specialist

Builds deep, self-directed expertise in a chosen domain and supplies it to the team. Currently: tested AI workflows for analytics delivery, with a validation protocol as the gate before anything ships.

How they interlock

Assessments are only useful if they predict behaviour. Together, these three describe a repeatable production line, and it is visible in every system on the Systems page.

GenerateIdeation proposes a better version of the workflow; Input supplies the collected reference material to build from.
FilterStrategic selects the route; Deliberative and Monitor Evaluator stress-test it for risk before anything is promised.
BuildImplementer converts the chosen design into a documented, versioned, runnable system rather than a one-off fix.
LandEmpathy and INFJ-A stakeholder reading make the system adopted, not just delivered: it is framed around what the audience needs to trust it.

The honest trade off in this profile: I evaluate before I cheer, so early silence can be mistaken for hesitance. It is not. It is the fine print being read, and it is why the things I ship tend to keep working.
