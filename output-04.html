The words on this page

  • Source. One file the week's page is written from. A row is cited by its id, a note or a log by its line number.
  • Reference. The source and the row or line a sentence rests on, in square brackets at the end of the sentence.
  • Claim. One checkable part of a sentence: a number, a name, an owner, a status, a date, a metric, a period or a change.
  • Check. The code compares every claim with the line it cites. A claim that does not hold there fails.
  • Removed. A sentence with a failed claim, or with anything checkable that cites nothing, is taken out whole and listed with its reason.
  • Declined. A source whose first line marks it confidential is not read, and nothing from it reaches the page.
  • Week. One folder of sources and the page released from it. The week ending date comes from the sources.
  • Run. One pass over every week in the test set by one model, recorded with its id, its tokens and its cost.
