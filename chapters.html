Notes from the road so far

Over the years I have written down what life has taught me, usually in a line or two, with a note on what I meant. These are my own, kept here so the collection can keep growing and I have something to look back on when I am older.

Each dot is a note, placed on the day it was written. Point at one to see its name.

Who you become

Meet your hero

Written 22 Jan 2026

They say don’t meet your heroes. I say don’t meet them unless that hero is you. Then it’s a must.

What it meansOften, when you meet your heroes, the actual person disappoints you. Turn the hero persona inwards, and you become the beacon for others rather than the other way round. Beacons are meant to be seen from afar, not too near.

More than one identity

Written 22 Jan 2026

Identity is what gives you purpose. Build yours so you become the person you look up to, and keep more than one.

What it meansAn identity is the start and end point of a purposeful journey: it tells you where you are and where you want to be. Having several means you are not stressed on one straight path. It lets you grow like life does, rather than set like concrete.

Many hats

Written 20 Aug 2026

Embrace wearing many hats. One person will always wear the most hats, and that person is a mother.

What it meansWearing many hats is another word for change, and the more you embrace it, the better you can regulate with it. A mother wears many hats whether she likes it or not, and nobody expects her to master it in a day; some take years and still need more time. Keep your heart open to it, because change is the one constant.

Circumstances

Written 6 Nov 2025

Change your circumstances before your circumstances change you.

What it meansChange what you can control while you still can, before the things you cannot control catch up and leave you less room to move.

How you prepare

Projected disadvantage

Written 13 Jan 2026 · pairs with Unfair advantage

Everyone has an unfair advantage. Not everyone has a projected disadvantage. Work with both, and never forget to project the disadvantages.

What it meansKnowing your advantages is one piece of the puzzle. The full picture comes when you project the disadvantages ahead of you, so you prepare for what may happen, never get too comfortable, and stay on your feet whatever the setting.

Min and max

Written 27 Oct 2024

Know the minimum that gets you back to a good baseline, and the maximum you would reach for.

What it meansWhen something knocks you down, the minimum is the least you need to get back to a good baseline. The maximum is the most you would do to get there, such as booking a hotel or taking a trip. You cannot reach for the maximum often: used too much, it stops serving its purpose and becomes too costly to keep up. The same goes for the minimum.

Mind, soul and body

Written 27 Oct 2024

Take care of your mind, soul and body, so you are not overwhelmed as you grow.

What it meansMind is work and continuous learning. Soul is reflection, hobbies, or anything that brings you peace, even rewatching your favourite shows. Body is exercise, or any activity that takes physical effort. Nurture all three, and your passage through life is kinder.

Respect time

Written 28 Apr 2026

If you respect time, time will respect you.

What it meansDon’t let time pass carelessly.

How you learn and act

The books you actually read

Written 20 Aug 2026 · pairs with Knowledge

The size of your library matters less than the books you actually read from it, often.

What it meansKnowledge is easy to acquire now, so quantity is no longer the measure; depth is. One book, prompt or skill that resonates can inspire you to write your own, iterate on it, and inspire others. That is the whole point of books: to inspire.

Get it, then act

Written 22 Jan 2026

It doesn’t matter how you narrate a concept. Once you get it, act on it, and finish. Action speaks louder than words.

What it meansYou don’t need to read every concept or watch every video. Get it and act on it, because you absorb more from doing. Diving deeper and deeper keeps the brain in learning mode instead of action mode, and drains the day’s battery. Choose what matters, and be active in your decisions.

Pilot and copilot

Written 9 Jul 2026

To pilot your Jaeger, the giant robot of Pacific Rim, you need to be in sync with your partner. Out of sync, you cannot pilot it alone.

What it meansThe Jaeger is the product you are building. Your partner is the AI you work with, and the two of you need to be in full sync. You stay the pilot and the AI is the copilot, and the copilot will always look to the pilot for direction.

No more can’t

Written 11 Jun 2026

In this modern world, there is no longer “I can’t”. There is only whether you want to do it.

What it meansWith AI, you have the power to do anything, so complaints should be kept to a minimum.

Moments and people

Find the window

Written 3 May 2026

Find that moment-filled window, be present, and don’t let the scenery pass. The window will close, and you will have to find it again.

What it meansTime will always pass, and moments pass with it, big or small. When you find yourself missing a time or a place, it is often because you were not present enough to soak it all in while it lasted. Be present, and there is less to miss later.

Kind over nice

Written 3 Mar 2025

Don’t be nice, be kind. The impact is bigger. Everyone can be nice; it takes a little more to be kind.

What it meansAnyone can say something quick: nice dress, nice hair. Kindness carries more weight, and it takes more to give.

Clear values

Written 2 May 2026

The early eras of Kamen Rider Kuuga, Ultraman Tiga and Ultraman Taro are golden because you never need a reason to understand the hero’s actions, like a child who cannot follow an adult’s words but understands an action that is pure.

What it meansThe golden days had clear values. Without them, everything twists in the wrong direction in the long run.

Dance with chaos

Written 15 Nov 2024

Learn how to dance with chaos. Once you do, you will be all the more content.

What it meansChaos will always be there, however hard you try to avoid it. So prepare for the next time you have to dance with it, and you can keep up with the moves it brings. Most times you still can’t keep up, and the dance leaves you tired. But once chaos leaves, you still learn from the dance. Chaos doesn’t stick like glue, and the better you keep up, the sooner the dance ends. So be ready to dance again when it comes.
