The words on this page

  • Transcript. The written record of one meeting, every spoken line numbered. The data is synthetic.
  • Row. One action in the tracker: who does it, what, by when, and the line it came from. A row is never rewritten by a later run.
  • Owner. The person named in the same sentence as the action, looked up in the contacts table. When no one is named, the owner is not stated, never guessed.
  • Due. The date as said in the meeting, turned into a calendar date from the meeting's own date. When it cannot be, it is kept as said.
  • Status. Open until done, closed when a later transcript says it happened, confirm while its date waits on a meeting date.
  • Gate. A point in the code where the run must stop and ask a person before it writes anything for that transcript.
  • Contacts table. A list of names, companies and roles that stands in for a CRM.
  • Run. One pass over the folder by one model, recorded with its id, its tokens and its cost.
